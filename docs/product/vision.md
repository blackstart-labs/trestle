# Product vision

Status: product direction; only the backend foundation is implemented.

Trestle is a cohesive self-hosted workspace in which teams plan projects, move
tasks through their workflows, record work, and connect the resulting knowledge.
The differentiator is usable, responsive everyday work with modest operational
complexity—not the number of modules or dashboard statistics.

## Product principles

1. One task identity across lists, boards, planning, time and links.
2. User-owned infrastructure and portable content; no proprietary service is
   required for core work.
3. Secure ownership and trustworthy totals before rich visualization.
4. Fast, bounded interactions, measured on reproducible deployment profiles.
5. Keyboard and touch workflows are first-class; dragging is never mandatory.
6. Dense, calm information hierarchy instead of decorative dashboards.
7. Introduce infrastructure and abstractions only for demonstrated needs.

## Initial audience and value

Independent developers, small product teams, consultants and small organizations
need one reliable place to connect planned work, actual time and documentation.
The first usable product must let an authorized workspace member manage a
project and its tasks end to end. It does not require graphs, invoicing,
structured collections, or workflow programming.

## Explicit non-goals for initial releases

- Feature parity with every reference product.
- Native apps, offline synchronization or collaborative document CRDTs.
- A generic user-programmable database or automation language.
- Dual interchangeable databases, microservices, Redis or search clusters.
- Financial/accounting compliance or guaranteed Android background uptime.

References inspire workflows; no external product code, schema, role hierarchy,
visual identity or benchmark is adopted as Trestle's specification.
